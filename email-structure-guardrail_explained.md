# How the Guardrail Works

The hook gives the recipient a reason to read, the proof supports that reason, and the pivot explains why the sender is writing now. Closing on one bounded question makes the requested response explicit. This ordering is an editorial preference, with no claim that it guarantees replies.

This skill owns structure. Existing outreach writers may still supply campaign strategy, and the selected voice supplies wording. When this skill is requested, its strict four-paragraph outreach format controls the body; a later explicit instruction can override it. Ordinary replies and governed application documents keep their own formats.

The recipient check runs before drafting. Unsupported achievements, referrals, availability, and urgency cannot be introduced to fill a stage. The humanizer improves the text within the structure, after which the agent reviews meaning and runs applicable voice checks. Missing required references must be disclosed rather than silently skipped.

The standard-library Python linter checks paragraph count, approximate sentence count, a final question, recognizable time limits, placeholders, several filler phrases, and em dashes. The separate semantic review checks evidence, relevance, and hidden extra asks. The regression suite includes a one-question double ask that passes punctuation checks to keep this limitation visible.

Install this folder as `email-structure-guardrail` in your assistant's skills directory.
